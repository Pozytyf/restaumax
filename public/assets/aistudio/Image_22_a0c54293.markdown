## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for the high-pressure, fast-paced environment of full-service restaurants, quick-service counters, and multi-location hospitality operations. The visual atmosphere balances operational authority with energetic hospitality. 

The personality is dependable, decisive, and kinetic. Interfaces must eliminate cognitive fatigue for cashiers during peak rush hours, clarify kitchen ticket states at a glance across steam and distance, and give managers instant oversight. 

The aesthetic blends **Modern SaaS Precision** with **Ergonomic Tactility**:
- Crisp, high-contrast typography designed for quick reading and rapid tapping.
- Clean structural surfaces in soft cool slate that prevent eye fatigue in dimly lit dining rooms or brightly lit service counters.
- Saturated, unmistakable action zones and status signals that guide physical touch interactions without hesitation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate operational hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#101D2D` - Deep Midnight Blue):** Anchors navigation rails, persistent headers, primary administrative text, and authoritative structural borders.
- **Secondary (`#FF6500` - Vibrant Orange):** The primary call-to-action engine. Reserved for direct conversion steps: "Valider l'encaissement", "Envoyer en cuisine", order creation triggers, and active live table indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F7FB` - Soft Grey Slate):** The primary application canvas, offering high comfort under screen glare. Surfaces stack pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards and panels on top of this tone.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for active cards, modal sheets, ticket surfaces, and POS keypad cells.

### Semantic Status Colors
- **Success / Ready / Paid (`#10B981`):** Settled accounts, table ready for seating, completed kitchen orders. Background tint: `#ECFDF5`.
- **Alert / Rush / Void / Out-of-Stock (`#EF4444`):** Delayed tickets, voided items, emergency kitchen stops, offline printers. Background tint: `#FEF2F2`.
- **Pending / In Kitchen / Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Orders firing, courses on hold, low stock warnings, cash drawer discrepancies. Background tint: `#FFFBEB`.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans powers all copy across POS terminals, kitchen display monitors, and mobile handheld waiter tablets. Its geometric balance and open counters deliver immediate legibility under harsh glare or rapid angled glances.

### Tabular Numbers & Financial Data
All monetary values (e.g., `14 500 FCFA`, `2 800 FCFA`) must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` (tabular figures). This guarantees strict vertical column alignment across cash receipts, checkout drawer totals, and split-bill lists. Currency indicators (`FCFA`) are rendered with a non-breaking space and set at `0.85em` relative scale or in medium weight to keep visual focus on the numeric total.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is optimized for persistent touch interactions and role-segregated workflows:

- **Desktop & Fixed Terminals (1024px+):** Employs an asymmetrical split view. A compact collapsible icon/label navigation bar (64px–240px) on the left, an order composition/category catalog area in the center (fluid 12-column grid, 16px gutter), and an anchor panel on the right (360px–420px fixed) dedicated to current order cart, discount modifiers, and immediate cashout buttons.
- **Tablet / Handheld POS (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. The live order total stays docked at the bottom as an expansive quick-touch drawer bar. Catalogs switch between 3-column and 4-column item grids with 12px gutters.
- **Mobile Waiter Devices (&lt; 768px):** 4-column flow with 8px gaps and 12px margins. Strict thumb-zone placement for the numpad and primary action buttons across the bottom 40% of the viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids excessive blur effects that degrade performance on low-spec Android POS hardware. Instead, depth is articulated through crisp tonal layering and subtle ambient boundaries:

- **Level 0 (Floor):** Canvas background `#F4F7FB`.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Category Tiles):** Background `#FFFFFF`, outline border `1px solid rgba(16, 29, 45, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Tickets, Draggable Tables, Focused Order Rows):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 29, 45, 0.12)`, shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(16, 29, 45, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Payment Splitters, Modifier Overlays):** Background `#FFFFFF`, shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(16, 29, 45, 0.14)`. Backdrop filter is kept to an opaque dimmed overlay (`rgba(16, 29, 45, 0.6)`) to ensure high contrast and rapid frame rates.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy leverages `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 8px / `0.5rem`):

- **Interactive Buttons & Input Fields:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for standard inputs, giving a crisp, solid target boundary.
- **Menu Item Tiles & Table Plan Nodes:** 12px to 16px (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) to look touch-friendly and distinct from flat sheet containers.
- **Status Badges & Quick Counter Chips:** Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to create clear distinction between content blocks and live state indicators.

## Components

### 1. Primary Touch Buttons (POS & Waiters)
- **Primary Checkout Button:** Minimum height `56px` on tablet/desktop, `52px` on mobile. Solid `#FF6500` background, `#FFFFFF` text in `label-lg`, rounded `10px`. Active touch feedback transitions background to `#E05800` without shift in layout.
- **Secondary Action Buttons:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #101D2D`, text `#101D2D`. Minimum height `48px`.
- **Quick Numpad Buttons:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#101D2D` (`currency-display-mobile`), border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, minimum height `64px` for error-free single-tap entry.

### 2. Status Badges & Ticket Indicators
- Rendered with `label-badge` uppercase typography, padding `4px 10px`, radius `9999px`.
- **Payé / Prêt:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`. Includes a 6px solid green dot before the label.
- **En Préparation / Attente:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`. Accompanied by elapsed kitchen timer text.
- **Annulé / Urgent / Rupture:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### 3. POS Cashout Cards & Item Tiles
- **Menu Item Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 29, 45, 0.08)`, radius `12px`. Features item title in `title-md`, price in `currency-table` using `#FF6500`, and quantity badges in top-right corner.
- **Receipt / Cart Line Item:** Alternating zero-state zebra striping on hovered rows, left border highlight in `#FF6500` when selected. Quantity modifiers (`+` / `-`) have minimum touch targets of `40px x 40px`.

### 4. Role-Adaptive Navigation
- **Floor Waiter View:** Distraction-free full-screen tables and seat matrices. Top bar with active server name, shift duration, and quick table switcher.
- **Cashier / POS View:** Fixed right-side ledger with grand total permanently locked in `#101D2D` container with `#FF6500` primary payment triggers.
- **Kitchen Display System (KDS):** High-contrast header ticket headers displaying order ticket numbers (`#101D2D`) with elapsed prep timers. Cards transition from neutral border to `#F59E0B` at 10 minutes, and flash `#EF4444` when exceeding 20 minutes.

### 5. Form & Numerical Inputs
- Numeric fields for custom FCFA entries must feature built-in clear triggers (`X`) with generous tap areas, auto-focusing on numpad activation.
